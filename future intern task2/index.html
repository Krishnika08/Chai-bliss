Our Story

A Legacy of Aromatic Excellence

Founded in Tirupur, Chai Bliss was born out of a passion for traditional brewing methods combined with modern café culture. We believe every cup tells a story, and we're here to make yours memorable.

From our hand-picked tea leaves to our expertly roasted coffee beans, quality is at the heart of everything we serve. Come experience the warmth of our hospitality and the richness of our flavors.
